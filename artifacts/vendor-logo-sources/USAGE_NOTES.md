# RnB Cloud vendor-logo usage notes

This pack is intended for the proposed **“Technologies We Work With”** website section.

## Important distinction

A technology logo can imply affiliation or endorsement. Do **not** rename the section “Our Partners,” “Strategic Partners,” “Official Partners,” or “Certified Partners” unless RnB Cloud has verified permission/relationship status for every vendor shown.

The downloader obtains current/reference artwork without modifying the trademarks. Before production use, compare each asset against the vendor's current brand guidance and RnB Cloud's own reseller/partner agreements.

## Vendor-specific flags

- **Microsoft — review before publishing.** Microsoft's current third-party logo guidance says a formal license is the general rule, with limited exceptions. If RnB Cloud's Microsoft reseller/CSP relationship qualifies, use the exact approved asset and treatment supplied for that relationship.
- **Cisco — permission/relationship logo recommended.** Cisco states its corporate logo is generally not granted to third parties; approved Cisco partner relationship assets are preferable when eligible.
- **Fortinet — use official kit/partner guidance.** Fortinet publishes a logo kit and trademark guidance. If RnB Cloud has a Fortinet partner relationship, use the approved partner treatment.
- **Google — follow current Google brand guidance.** Verify any Google Workspace reseller/partner rights before production use.
- **NinjaOne — official media kit available.** Use the current official media kit/brand guide treatment.
- **SentinelOne — do not alter.** SentinelOne's brand page identifies its graphics as proprietary and says not to alter them.
- **Datto — verify current Kaseya/Datto treatment.** Datto is a Kaseya company; use whatever current reseller/MSP branding is permitted.
- **Jamf — official media kit available.** Prefer the correct approved partner asset when applicable.

## Developer recommendation

Keep logos subordinate to the RnB Cloud brand, use consistent optical height rather than identical width, preserve aspect ratio, and never recolor or modify a vendor trademark unless that vendor's guidelines explicitly allow it.
